# CAD and Resources for Silicon Nitride Shadow Masking of Released Structures

## Files in this repository:

- ```shadowmask_LN_evap_fixture.f3z```: CAD and CNC toolpaths for the three-part fixture used to secure the shadow mask and target chip during flip-chip bond alignment, bonding, and evaporation. This was machined from 6061 aluminum.
- ```shadowmask_spacer_metallization_fixture.f3d```: CAD for a fixture to hold the 4" shadow mask wafer and mechanical stop metallization mask during deposition. This was machined from 6061 aluminum.
- ```template_shadowmask_wafer.gds```: Reference CAD for the 4" shadow mask wafer and the mechanical stop metallization mask. The mask was fabricated from laser-cut 4 mil stainless steel sheet.

-  ```shadowmask_quadrant_cleaver.f3d```: CAD for a 3D FDM printed tool to cleave a quadrant of the 4" shadow mask wafer into individual chips following KOH etching and mechanical spacer deposition. This was printed from PETG.
- ```shadowmask_wafer_cleaver.f3d```: CAD for a 3D FDM printed tool to cleave a 4" shadow mask wafer into individual quadrants following KOH etching and mechanical spacer deposition. This was printed from PETG.
